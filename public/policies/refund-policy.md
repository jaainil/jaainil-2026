## 2. Professional Services & Custom Software Development

Because custom software development and DevOps engineering require substantial dedicated time, intellectual effort, and custom configuration:

### A. Fixed-Price Milestone Projects
For projects structured with fixed milestone payments:
1. **Initial Project Deposit / Kickoff Milestone:** An upfront deposit (typically 25% to 50% of the total contract value) is required to secure project schedule, conduct technical discovery, configure cloud infrastructure, and begin architecture design. This deposit is **non-refundable** once work on the milestone has commenced.
2. **Milestone Review & Acceptance Window:** Upon delivery of each milestone (e.g., database schema, API implementation, containerized Docker Compose stack, or UI frontend), the client has **seven (7) calendar days** to inspect the deliverable and verify that it matches agreed technical specifications.
3. **Revisions & Corrections:** If the deliverable contains functional defects or fails to meet the written specifications, we will provide necessary revisions at no additional charge within the agreed scope.
4. **Milestone Completion:** Once a milestone is approved by the client (or if no feedback is provided within the 7-day review window), the milestone is deemed accepted, and the associated payment becomes final and **non-refundable**.
5. **Cancellation of Subsequent Milestones:** If either party chooses to terminate the contract prior to completion, the client is under no obligation to pay for subsequent, uncommenced milestones. Any prepaid funds for milestones where work has not yet started will be promptly refunded within **fourteen (14) business days**.

### B. Time & Materials / Hourly Engagements
For consulting, incident response, DevOps troubleshooting, or hourly engineering:
- Invoices are generated based on actual logged and verified working hours.
- Payments for hours already worked and delivered are **non-refundable**.
- If a retainer is paid in advance, any unused retainer balance at the time of written project termination will be refunded to the client within **14 business days**.
